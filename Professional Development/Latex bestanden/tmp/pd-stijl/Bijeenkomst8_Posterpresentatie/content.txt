8 #2 The poster should contain scientific results;  Memo’s & presentaties.The poster should be maximally A0 in portrait;  Dit bepalen wij.Make sure the poster;:- refers to the people involved;  Foto van jou/jullie. Namen die hebben bijgedragen aan jullie project moeten erbij.- includes their institutions/organizations;  UVA/HvA/NIKHEF/etc.- and is accompanied by the appropriate acknowledgements, logos etc.  Logo UVA/HvA/NIKHEFLaat je poster controleren bij jouw (wetenschappelijke) begeleider!
